Delete your ClickSwapp account

The quickest way is in the app: Settings → Delete my account. If you can't open the app, ask us by email.

Delete it by email

  1. Write to us

    Compose an email to support@clickswapp.com.

  2. Add the subject

    In the subject line, write "Account Deletion Request".

  3. Say which account

    In the email body, include your email address and a clear request for account deletion. Send it from the same address.

  4. Wait for our reply

    Send the email and wait for our team to confirm that the account has been deleted.

Email an account deletion request

Please note

  • Account deletion is irreversible. Your personal information is permanently removed from our system.
  • You choose what happens to the photos you sent: leave them in the exchange, or remove them from it. People who have already received your photos keep them.
  • Photos that violate the Terms and Conditions are removed for everyone.
