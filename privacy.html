Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

At ClickSwapp, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app

1. Information We Collect

  • Emails: We collect your email address to help with account management and communication.
  • Location of Photos: We gather the location of your photos. The app rounds the coordinates to about 110 meters before sending them, and we store what we receive. We turn the location into a place name (country, region, city) using the open GeoNames data.
  • Photos: Photos you upload are stored on our servers so they can be exchanged with other users. Before a photo is shown to anyone else, it is checked for explicit content (e.g., pornographic material) by an automated classifier and reviewed by our moderators.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • We use your email address for account-related communication.
  • We use the location of your photos to show where each photo was taken. The person who receives your photo sees the place name and the approximate location of the photo (accurate to about 110 meters), including on their map. You see the places your photos were delivered to as a city or an approximate area, without any information about the recipient.
  • Photos are checked for explicit content, such as pornography, and reviewed by our moderators before they are shown to other users. This is done to ensure the safety and appropriateness of shared content.

3. Data Retention

  • We retain your email address for as long as your account is active or as needed for legal purposes.
  • The location of a photo is stored together with the photo. It is not used for any other purpose and is not shared with third parties.
  • Photos are stored on our servers to provide the photo exchange, as described in the Terms and Conditions. The moderation result is stored together with the photo.

4. Data Sharing

  • We do not share your personal information, including email or photo location data, with third parties, except as required by law.
  • Photos are processed on our own servers, including moderation, and are not sent to third-party services.

5. Security

  • We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
  • Photos are transmitted securely during moderation.

6. Your Rights

  • You can update or delete your account information at any time.
  • You can contact us at support@clickswapp.com to inquire about or request deletion of your data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

  • We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in the app, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

By using our app, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at support@clickswapp.com
